Overview

This study proposes a model for extending a construction cost-estimation and progress-payment document ontology from one trade to another. Using an ontology developed for reinforced-concrete work as the source structure, the model extracts and aligns concepts from tile-work documents, identifies extension candidates, and places them within the ontology using document evidence and WBS hierarchy information.

Key Findings

  • Extended the ontology from 31 to 48 classes, from 55 to 62 properties, and from 486 to 557 schema triples while preserving the common cost structure.
  • Added tile-specific material and installation cost concepts, including ceramic, porcelain, mosaic, and non-slip tile cost items.
  • HermiT and OWLAPI validation confirmed logical consistency, zero unsatisfiable classes, and zero OWL 2 profile violations.
